# Levara Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 1, 2026**

Levara is a non-commercial student project built by a small group of students ("we", "us"). It is used for learning and research on health and fitness data. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the general public.

## Who can use Levara

Only members of the project team, and people who personally choose to take part, can connect an account or provide data. Each person provides only their own data and does so voluntarily. Participants must be 18 or older.

## Data sources and what we access

Levara can bring in data from the following sources. Participants choose which, if any, to connect.

| Source | How the data arrives | Data categories |
|---|---|---|
| **Apple Health** | The participant exports their Health data from the Apple Health app and gives us the export file | Workouts, heart rate, steps, activity and energy, distance, sleep, and other health measurements contained in the export |
| **Oura** | The participant authorizes Levara through Oura's login (OAuth) | Sleep, readiness, daily activity, heart rate, SpO2, workouts, and related profile information |
| **WHOOP** | The participant authorizes Levara through WHOOP's login (OAuth) | Workouts, sleep, recovery (score, resting heart rate, HRV, SpO2, skin temperature), daily strain, body measurements, and basic profile information |
| **Strava** | The participant authorizes Levara through Strava's login (OAuth) | Activities such as runs and rides (type, time, duration, distance, heart rate, and similar details) and basic profile information |

We access only the categories above, only for accounts whose owners have authorized us, and only the data permitted by the permissions (scopes) they approve. Participants can see those permissions on each provider's authorization screen.

## How the data is used

The data is used only for the project's learning and research purposes, such as viewing and analyzing health and fitness trends. It is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling or automated decision-making, and it is not sold or rented. Data from a participant's provider account is shown only to that participant and to project team members.

## Storage, access and security

Data is stored in a database on computers controlled by the project team. It is accessible only to project team members, and access tokens are kept in private configuration that is not published. We do not share participants' data with third parties and we do not publish it in identifiable form. Data is not stored in iCloud.

While we develop and test, a temporary network connection service may relay traffic between a provider and our software. It passes data through but is not used to store it.

## Third-party providers

Each provider (Apple, Oura, WHOOP, Strava) has its own terms and privacy policy that apply to the data you hold with them. This policy covers only what Levara does with data after you have authorized it. Levara is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Apple, Oura, WHOOP or Strava.

## Retention and deletion

We keep a participant's data only while the project is active. Any participant can ask us to delete their data at any time by emailing the address below, and can revoke Levara's access at any time from the provider's settings (the Oura, WHOOP or Strava account or app settings). We will delete the data we hold for that participant, including data from any provider, within 30 days of a request. Apple Health export files that were shared with us are deleted at the same time.

## Children

Levara is not directed at anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
